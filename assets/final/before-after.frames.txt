title: before after

[frame 1]
The switch is open, no current is flowing. The relay is open, and there is a break in the circuit.

[frame 2]
The switch is closed, current is flowing. The relay is now closed, and the light bulb is powered because there is a difference in voltage.

[frame 3]
The switch is open, no current is flowing. The relay is not pulling, so the arm is touching the `-` side and driving the output wire low.

[frame 4]
The switch is closed, current is flowing. The relay has pulled the arm, so it is touching the `+` side and driving the output wire high.
