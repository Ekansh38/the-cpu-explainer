title: before after

[frame 1]
The switch is open, so the coil is off. The relay is open, and there is a break in the circuit.

[frame 2]
The switch is closed, so the coil is on. The relay is now closed, so the loop is complete and the light bulb is on.

[frame 3]
The switch is open, so the coil is off. The relay is not pulling, so the arm rests on the `-` side and is actively driving the output wire low.

[frame 4]
The switch is closed, so the coil is on. The relay has pulled the arm, so it rests on the `+` side and is actively driving the output wire high.
