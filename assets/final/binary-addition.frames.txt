title: binary addition

[frame 1]
We want to add 7 and 3 in binary, that's `0111` plus `0011`. We go column by column, right to left, just like normal addition.

[frame 2]
First column: 1 plus 1 is 2, which is `10` in binary. We write down 0 and carry the 1.

[frame 3]
Next column: 1 plus 1 plus the carried 1 is 3, which is `11`. We write down 1 and carry the 1 again.

[frame 4]
Next column: 1 plus 0 plus the carried 1 is 2, so we write down 0 and carry the 1.

[frame 5]
Last column: 0 plus 0 plus the carried 1 is 1, so we just write down 1.

[frame 6]
The result is `1010`, which is 10. And sure enough, 7 plus 3 is 10.
