title: 8-bit adder examples

[frame 1]
`A` is 42 and `B` is 5, and the output is 47. No `Carry Out`, since the result fits in 8 bits.

[frame 2]
`A` is 15 and `B` is 1, and the output is 16.

[frame 3]
`A` is 255 and `B` is 1. 255 is the largest number 8 bits can hold, so adding 1 overflows: the output wraps around to 0 and the `Carry Out` turns on.
