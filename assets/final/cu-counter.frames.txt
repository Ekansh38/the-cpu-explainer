title: cu counter

[frame 1]
The counter is sitting at 0, so the decoder has output 0 on, which is connected to nothing. `CLK` is currently off.

[frame 2]
`CLK` turns on, and on that rising edge the accumulator counts up to 1. `Q0` turns on, and the decoder switches to output 1. This is step 1.

[frame 3]
`CLK` turns back off. Nothing happens, the counter only captures on the rising edge, so it just keeps holding 1.

[frame 4]
`CLK` on again, the counter captures 2. `Q1` is on, `Q0` is off, binary for 2. The decoder moves to output 2.

[frame 5]
`CLK` off, holding 2.

[frame 6]
Rising edge, count up to 3. `Q1` and `Q0` on.

[frame 7]
Holding 3.

[frame 8]
Rising edge, count up to 4. Only `Q2` on now, binary for 4.

[frame 9]
Holding 4.

[frame 10]
Rising edge, count up to 5.

[frame 11]
Holding 5.

[frame 12]
Rising edge, count up to 6. We are in the unused steps now, these output wires still turn on, they just aren't connected to anything yet.

[frame 13]
Holding 6.

[frame 14]
Rising edge, count up to 7. All three `Q` wires on, the counter is full.

[frame 15]
Holding 7.

[frame 16]
Rising edge, and 7 + 1 overflows! The counter wraps right back around to 0, no reset wire needed. The whole loop just starts over, forever.
