title: sr latch

[frame 1]
Both inputs to the bottom `NOR` gate are off, so its output, `NOT Q`, is on. Since the output of the bottom `NOR` gate is on, the top `NOR` gate's output, `Q`, has to be off. The latch is holding a `0`.

[frame 2]
Pressing `SET` forces `NOT Q` to be off, allowing the top `NOR` gate to output true, because its two inputs are off. The latch is now holding a `1`.

[frame 3]
Now `SET` is off, but the bottom `NOR` gate's output stays off, because the top `NOR` gate's output is an input into the bottom one, forcing it off. The loop holds itself up with nothing pressed. That is the bit being remembered.

[frame 4]
`RESET` forces `Q` off, allowing `NOT Q` to be on, just like `SET` but in reverse. Let go of `RESET` and we are back to frame 1, holding a `0`.
