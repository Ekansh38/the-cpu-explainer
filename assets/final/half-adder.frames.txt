title: half adder

[frame 1]
`A` and `B` are both 0, so the `XOR` outputs a `sum` of 0 and the `AND` outputs a `carry-out` of 0. 0 plus 0 is 0.

[frame 2]
`A` is 1 and `B` is 0. The `XOR` has two different inputs, so the `sum` is 1. The `AND` doesn't have both inputs on, so the `carry-out` is 0. 1 plus 0 is 1.

[frame 3]
`A` is 0 and `B` is 1. Same thing, the `XOR` gives a `sum` of 1 and the `AND` gives a `carry-out` of 0. 0 plus 1 is 1.

[frame 4]
`A` and `B` are both 1. The `XOR` has two matching inputs, so the `sum` is 0. But now the `AND` has both inputs on, so the `carry-out` is 1. 1 plus 1 is `10` in binary, a `sum` of 0 and a `carry-out` of 1.
