title: full accumulator

[frame 1]
`STEP` is off. The register holds 0, and `B` is fixed at 1. The adder is adding them, so a 1 is waiting at the register's `D` inputs.

[frame 2]
`STEP` is pressed. On that rising edge, the register captures the 1 that was waiting at its `D` inputs.

[frame 3]
`STEP` is still held, but nothing new happens. The register holds 1, and that 1 loops back into the adder, which starts working out the next number, 2.

[frame 4]
`STEP` is off, holding 1, with the 2 waiting at the inputs.

[frame 5]
`STEP` is pressed again. On the rising edge, the register captures the 2 that was waiting at its inputs.

[frame 6]
`STEP` is still held, but nothing new happens. The register holds 2, that 2 loops back, and the adder starts on 3.

[frame 7]
`STEP` is off, holding 2, with 3 waiting at the inputs. Every press of `STEP` adds 1, so this circuit counts up.
