title: full accumulator

[frame 1]
`STEP` is off. The register holds 0, and `B` is fixed at 1. The adder is adding them, so a 1 is waiting at the register's `D` inputs.

[frame 2]
`STEP` is pressed. This is when the calculation happens, the adder works out 0 plus 1, and the result lands at the register's inputs.

[frame 3]
`STEP` is still held, and now the register stores that result, so it holds 1. That 1 loops back into the adder, which starts working out the next number, 2.

[frame 4]
`STEP` is off, holding 1, with the 2 waiting at the inputs.

[frame 5]
`STEP` is pressed again. The calculation happens, the adder works out 1 plus 1.

[frame 6]
`STEP` is still held, and the register stores the 2. That 2 loops back, and the adder starts on 3.

[frame 7]
`STEP` is off, holding 2, with 3 waiting at the inputs. Every press of `STEP` adds 1, so this circuit counts up.
