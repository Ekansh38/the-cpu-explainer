title: 2 4 decoder

[frame 1]
The input into the decoder is `00`, which is the number 0. That is why the output 0 is on, the bottom-most wire.

[frame 2]
The input into the decoder is `01`, which is the number 1. That is why the output 1 is on, the second wire from the bottom.

[frame 3]
The input into the decoder is `10`, which is the number 2. That is why the output 2 is on, the third wire from the bottom.

[frame 4]
The input into the decoder is `11`, which is the number 3. That is why the output 3 is on, the top-most wire.
