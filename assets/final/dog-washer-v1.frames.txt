title: dog washer v1

[frame 1]
`MUDDY`, `STINKY`, and `OLD_WASH` are all off. With nothing switched on, no current reaches the bulb, so `WASH` is off.

[frame 2]
`MUDDY` is switched on, so its coil powers up and starts pulling its relay.

[frame 3]
`MUDDY`'s relay has been pulled, which satisfies the `MUDDY OR STINKY` part and powers the next relay down the chain. But `OLD_WASH` is still off, so `WASH` stays off.

[frame 4]
The signal keeps propagating down the chain toward the final `AND`. Still, `OLD_WASH` is off, so `WASH` is off.

[frame 5]
Now `OLD_WASH` is switched on too. Both inputs to the final `AND` are on, so its relay powers up and starts pulling.

[frame 6]
That last relay closes, completing the circuit. `(MUDDY OR STINKY) AND OLD_WASH` is now true, so `WASH` turns on.

[frame 7]
`STINKY` is switched on as well. It doesn't change anything, `MUDDY` already satisfied the OR, so `WASH` stays on.

[frame 8]
`STINKY`'s relay pulls too, so now both `MUDDY` and `STINKY` feed the OR. `WASH` is still on.
