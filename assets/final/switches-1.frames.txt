title: switches 1

[frame 1]
`STINKY` & `OLD_WASH` are both open, thus no current can flow. That is why the bulb is off.

[frame 2]
`STINKY` is closed but `OLD_WASH` is open, thus no current can flow. That is why the bulb is off.

[frame 3]
`STINKY` & `OLD_WASH` are both closed, thus current can flow. That is why the bulb is on.

[frame 4]
`STINKY` & `OLD_WASH` are both open, thus no current can flow. That is why the bulb is off.

[frame 5]
`OLD_WASH` is closed but `STINKY` is open, thus no current can flow. That is why the bulb is off.

[frame 6]
`STINKY` & `OLD_WASH` are both closed, thus current can flow. That is why the bulb is on.
