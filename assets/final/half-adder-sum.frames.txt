title: half adder sum

[frame 1]
`A` and `B` are both 0. The `OR` outputs 0 and the `NAND` outputs 1, but the final `AND` needs both inputs on, so the output is 0.

[frame 2]
`A` is 1 and `B` is 0. Now the `OR` outputs 1, and the `NAND` outputs 1 since its inputs aren't both on. Both inputs of the final `AND` are on, so the output is 1.

[frame 3]
`A` is 0 and `B` is 1. Same thing, the `OR` outputs 1 and the `NAND` outputs 1, so the final `AND` outputs 1.

[frame 4]
`A` and `B` are both 1. The `OR` still outputs 1, but now the `NAND` has both inputs on, so it outputs 0. The final `AND` loses one of its inputs, so the output is 0.
